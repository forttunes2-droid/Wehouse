# Pro growth and hotel refund implementation
Worker Pro adds job costs and net after recorded costs, based on released Worker earnings, plus CSV export for the latest 150 eligible jobs. Blank cost is unrecorded, not zero. This does not change protected payments, tax accounting or payout balances. Calendar export contains job references and services without customer contact data; it is a snapshot requiring re-export after changes.
Partner Pro adds repeating maintenance tasks (1–365 day intervals) and portfolio calendar export. Completing a repeat creates exactly one next occurrence; overdue tasks restart from today. Reopening a predecessor after its next occurrence exists is rejected. These features do not assign staff or block inventory.
Next larger needs: background reminder delivery with tested notification routing, availability closures integrated with inventory, scoped team assignment, booking-linked turnover checklists, and consented customer follow-up. These require dedicated implementation and operational validation, and are not advertised as included.
Hotel paid cancellation now snapshots exact hotel-local refund terms, permits eligible full cancellation before check-in, records an immutable financial outbox obligation and holds protected money. Inventory releases through cancelled status. Existing bookings without exact snapshots require review. Refund status remains pending/processing/review until verified refund.processed posts the compensating ledger and updates payment state. Provider uncertainty uses existing manual review rather than blind resubmission. Signed refund events reconcile the outbox, including the response/webhook race.
This implementation still requires real Paystack sandbox, hosted concurrent cancellation/payment tests, local-time boundaries and Nigeria-specific legal review before production rollout. These cannot be represented by passing mocked browser tests. No production deployment or merge is performed.
