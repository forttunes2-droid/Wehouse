## Status meanings

- **Confirmed direction:** explicitly stated by the product owner in the requests that led to this audit; implementation still requires migration/testing.
- **Repository-established:** current implementation is coherent enough to document as the baseline, subject to regression tests.
- **Open:** repository contains conflicting behavior or does not establish a trustworthy answer; owner approval is required.

## C-055 Regional layer (currency, time zone, date)
- `src/lib/regional.ts` is the only place that knows the default country. Defaults load from `platform_countries` at app start; the built-in fallback keeps the app working before it loads.
- Moments (payments, deadlines) show in the viewer's time zone with the zone name. Property times (check-in, handover) use the property's own zone. Calendar dates are never shifted.
- Money is shown in the currency it was charged in, formatted for the viewer. No ₦, `en-NG` or `Africa/Lagos` literals in `src` outside the regional layer; `tests/regional-layer.test.mjs` enforces this.

## 25 October owner correction: full Year 1 rent

- One-year Long Let is always paid in full before handover. There is no one-year monthly-payment option. Accepted shorter terms are also full-payment terms.
- For a multi-year contract, the landlord may accept monthly payments for later years. Year 1 remains paid in full. Each later year's rent is paid in eight amounts during months 5–12 of the preceding year, starting four months after handover.
- Without landlord opt-in, collect the full multi-year contract amount upfront. Keep the accepted amount, term and schedule together in the existing reservation and rent records.
