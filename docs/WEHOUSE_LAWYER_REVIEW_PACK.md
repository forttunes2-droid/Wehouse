# WeHouse Lawyer Review Pack

**Status:** Internal review brief — not public terms.
**Product territory assumed for this review:** Nigeria-first launch.
**Prepared:** 13 September 2026.
**Important:** Product and engineering draft; counsel must give the legal conclusions.

## 1. Product counsel is reviewing

WeHouse combines:

- Long Let apartment reservation, optional inspection, rent payment and physical handover;
- date-based Short Let reservation, arrival/check-in, checkout and payment handling;
- hotel discovery, inventory, payment, hotel-team check-in/out and guest messages;
- roommate discovery/matching and private communications;
- free Worker onboarding, service booking, protected payment, completion, dispute and verified-job reviews;
- optional paid Worker professional features, web Paystack billing and native-store billing gates;
- Property Partner, Hotel and scoped staff/admin/creator workspaces;
- support cases, messaging, media, location, identity/authority evidence and analytics.

## 2. Decisions already fixed in product design

These should be changed only if counsel identifies a legal requirement:

1. Accounts are 18+; date of birth is private eligibility data, not public profile data.
2. Free Workers remain fully usable. Paid status never equals Reviewed, Trusted, licensed or endorsed.
3. Reviewed/Trusted are earned under disclosed checks. Customer ratings require a completed paid WeHouse job.
4. Short Let public location shows a useful street/area; entrance, unit/access instructions, codes and precise directions remain gated.
5. Payment is authoritative only after server verification. Conflicts freeze the affected flow for review.
6. Short Let occupancy belongs to a dated reservation, not the listing’s global availability.
7. A conversation remains attached to one purpose and does not replace a formal dispute/payment record.
8. Paid-plan monthly/yearly prices and benefits are dashboard/store configuration, not hard-coded marketing claims.

## 3. Legal classification and approvals required

Counsel should give a written answer, responsible regulator, filing/approval, launch condition and renewal duty for each row.

| Area | Question counsel must decide | Product launch consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate/tax | Correct entity, objects, CAC filings, tax/VAT/withholding and invoice duties | Insert real entity details; configure receipts/tax |
| Worker marketplace | Is WeHouse only a marketplace for independent providers, or do matching, price/payment/control activities require a private employment/recruiter licence? | Gate Worker marketplace until written classification and any NELEX approval |
| Property agency | Do listing, reservation, rent collection, inspection or handover activities require estate agency or State-specific registration? | Define disclosed role and licensed operating model |
| Short Let/hospitality | Which State/LGA hospitality, premises, tourism, fire, health or local permits apply to WeHouse and each property? | Partner onboarding evidence and territory launch gate |
| Hotels/PMS | Required hotel licences, guest registers, identity reporting, tourism/security rules, PMS processing terms | Certification checklist before a hotel goes live |
| Payments | Is WeHouse a merchant/marketplace using a licensed processor, or carrying on regulated payment/escrow/wallet activity? | No unlicensed custody; processor settlement and ledger wording must match opinion |
| Consumer protection | Cancellation, refund, price display, deposit, auto-renewal, complaint and unfair-term compliance | Approve product-specific refund/cancellation schedules |
| Data protection | Controller/processor roles, NDPC registration/class, DPIA, DPO, breach process, transfers, children/age data | Privacy programme and signed processor agreements before launch |
| Identity/biometrics | Lawful basis, necessity, retention and vendor terms for identity and any facial/liveness checks | Keep feature gated until DPIA and approval |
| Communications | Email/SMS/push/marketing consent and opt-out rules | Separate transactional from marketing messages |
| Regulated Workers | How medical, electrical, security and other regulated categories are verified and described | Category gates; do not imply a licence without source verification |
| Security services | Whether any listed guarding/security service requires NSCDC licensing/approval | Block the category until approved |
| Promotions/Sponsored | Disclosure and any FCCPC promotion registration for discounts, ranking or competitions | “Sponsored” label and campaign approval path |
| IP/content | User media licence, takedown, music/GIF/sticker provider rights | No unlicensed GIF/sticker catalogue or music library |
| Insurance | Whether protection wording amounts to insurance or a guarantee | Use only approved wording/product structure |

## 5. Exact questions requiring written advice

1. Confirm whether “18 or older for all accounts” is lawful and sufficient, what evidence may be collected, and how existing accounts without recorded age must be migrated.
2. Confirm the legal identity of each contracting party for Long Let, Short Let, hotel and Worker bookings.
3. Confirm whether the reservation fee is refundable, credited toward the main price, or separately earned, and at exactly which event.
4. Approve a stage-based cancellation/refund table for each product; do not approve a blanket no-refund term.
5. Confirm who legally holds customer money, when each supplier earns it, and whether the current “protected” wording is permissible.
6. Confirm that Long Let remains rent-only with no security deposit, and approve any separately disclosed Short Let payment/refund rule.
7. Confirm Worker marketplace licensing and the boundary between independent marketplace, recruitment and employment.
8. Confirm required property/hotel licences per launch State/LGA and the evidence WeHouse must retain.
9. Confirm app-store/web billing disclosures, auto-renewal consent, cancellation and price-change notice.
10. Confirm Sponsored placement disclosures and whether paid ordering may be mixed with organic ranking.
11. Confirm retention periods for DOB eligibility, IDs/biometrics, property evidence, payment records, chats, calls, reviews, location, disputes and security logs.
12. Approve dispute escalation, governing law and forum without restricting mandatory FCCPC/data-subject rights.

## 7. Release rule

Do not enable a regulated surface merely because code exists. Each production gate should record the approving authority, document/evidence reference, effective date, territory/category scope, expiry/renewal date and responsible owner. Counsel’s approval should be attached to that release record. Features without required approval remain visible only in authorised test environments or disabled.
